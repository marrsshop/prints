Tim Marrs is a digital illustrator and art director for motion, known for his distinctive, highly recognisable visual style.

His work spans editorial, publishing, advertising and music, with an international client list that includes Ogilvy & Mather, Saatchi & Saatchi, M&C Saatchi, BBH, Nike, Audi, The Telegraph, Billboard, the Financial Times, Rolling Stone and Geffen Records.

Working across still and moving image, Marrs brings a bold, energetic and graphic approach to each project, combining illustration, art direction and sometimes motion to create visually striking work.
